Why I'm writing this blog

ASHWIN JUL 26, 2026 3 MIN READ

I'm starting this as a first-year Electrical Engineering student at San José State University, mostly because I keep finding that I don't really understand a power systems or power electronics topic until I've tried to explain it in plain language. This blog is that process, made public.

What this is

Feeder Notes is a running notebook on power electronics and power systems — converters, transformers, protection, the grid, and the semiconductors underneath all of it. Some posts will be me working through a textbook concept for the first time. Others will be notes from a project, a datasheet I've been staring at, or something I built on a bench. I'd rather write things down clearly while I'm still learning them than wait until I feel like an expert.
